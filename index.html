SERVICES

End-to-End Mobile Development Services

From initial wireframes and game design documents to polished production releases on the Apple App Store and Google Play Store.

Mobile Game Development

Full-cycle 2D and 3D game creation for iOS and Android. We specialize in casual games, roguelikes, action runners, and tactical strategy with addictive core gameplay loops and balanced progression.

  • Unity Engine & C# Architecture
  • Physics-based mechanics & animations
  • Save states, cloud sync & offline play

Custom Mobile Applications

High-performance, secure, and intuitive utility, business, and entertainment apps. Built with Flutter, React Native, or Native platforms to ensure smooth 60 FPS transitions and rapid time-to-market.

  • Cross-platform iOS & Android codebases
  • Real-time push notifications & background tasks
  • Local storage & offline-first data sync

UI/UX & Interactive Design

Visual design that converts. We craft ergonomic interfaces, polished game HUDs, visual effects (VFX), responsive touch controls, and cohesive design systems tailored for mobile ergonomics.

  • Interactive prototypes in Figma
  • Custom game UI, icons & sprite assets
  • Smooth micro-animations & tactile haptics

Cloud Backend & Multiplayer

Scalable serverless backends, real-time multiplayer networking, global leaderboards, authentication, and secure databases powered by Firebase, AWS, and RESTful microservices.

  • Firebase Firestore, Auth & Cloud Functions
  • Live multiplayer & competitive leaderboards
  • Automated analytics & crash telemetry

Monetization & Ad Mediation

Maximize revenue without degrading player retention. We implement seamless In-App Purchases (IAP), rewarded video ads, interstitial ad mediation, and battle pass/subscription architectures.

  • Google AdMob, Unity Ads & AppLovin MAX
  • Apple StoreKit 2 & Google Play Billing
  • A/B testing for pricing & ad placements

Publishing, QA & ASO

Hassle-free release lifecycle. We handle Apple App Store guidelines, Google Play target API compliance, automated device testing, battery profiling, and App Store Optimization (ASO).

  • Compliance review & metadata optimization
  • Multi-device resolution & screen adaptation
  • Crash-rate reduction & memory leak profiling
