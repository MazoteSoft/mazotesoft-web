Sliding Lateral Niches: Special & Mythic Sanctuaries

Legendary cards transcend the physical bounds of the monolith. Whenever an ancient relic or reality-bending sorcery is grabbed, these concealed wings slide dynamically from the screen borders—crafted in gold-veined black marble exclusively for Tier 3 Founders.

🟣 Left Lateral Niche • Special Cards

Amethyst Sorcery Sanctuary

Emerges smoothly from the left edge of the screen whenever you grab one of the 10 Special Cards. Acting as a dedicated arcane sanctuary, it executes reality-altering board effects without occupying standard altar slots.

  • ✦ Auto-Slide Trigger: Slides into the board in 180ms upon touching cards like Absolute Zero, Arcane Magnet, Dark Fog, or Mirror Dimension.
  • ✦ Slot Preservation: Casts field-wide mutators and crowd control while keeping your three core Attack, Defense, and Utility slots unblocked.
  • ✦ Founder Aesthetics (Tier 3): Monolithic black marble with radiant gold veining, a floating icosahedral amethyst crystal, and violet Futhark runic engravings.
🟡 Right Lateral Niche • Mythic Cards

Solar Relic Citadel

Summoned from the right edge of the arena the instant you touch one of the 5 Mythic Cards. Channels supreme celestial energy into your run, granting massive score bursts and charging your Frenzy Meter.

  • ✦ Auto-Slide Trigger: Slides into view when dragging Astral Relic, Loot Goblin, Time Shard, Astral Specter, or Alchemical Bomb.
  • ✦ Frenzy Power Surge: Dropping a mythic card unleashes a runic shockwave that immediately awards +5 bonus charges to your Frenzy Meter.
  • ✦ 1.25x Fall Velocity: Mythic cards fall 25% faster than standard cards at any given moment, demanding razor-sharp reflexes to catch.
  • ✦ Founder Aesthetics (Tier 3): Hand-carved obsidian marble adorned with a golden solar halo medallion, incandescent amber runes, and braided Celtic knotwork columns.

TACTICAL DRAFT MUTATORS • 30 RUNIC GIFTS

The 30 Norse Blessings

Sculpt your run's strategy between levels. Draft divine permanent modifiers that alter board physics, grant invulnerability shields, accelerate frenzy multipliers, and forge high-score synergies.

⚖️

Universal 2-Level Duration & Activation Guarantee

Standard Duration: Every chosen blessing remains active for 2 full levels across your run.

First-Use Guarantee (Protection): If a blessing's condition does not manifest during those 2 levels (e.g. Combo Shield when no error occurred, or Elastic Bounce when no card touched the abyss), it never expires. It stays active indefinitely until triggered for the first time!
