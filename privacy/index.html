Privacy Policy

Publisher: MazoteSoft • Applications Covered: Rogue Draft (com.roguedraft.game), LottoCol, and studio mobile apps • Last Updated: October 14, 2026

Summary: MazoteSoft respects your privacy. We do not sell your personal data. We collect minimal technical and gameplay telemetry to operate online leaderboards, display ads via Google AdMob, and process in-app purchases through Google Play.

1. Introduction and Scope

This Privacy Policy applies to the mobile games, applications, and related services operated by MazoteSoft ("we", "us", or "our"), including but not limited to Rogue Draft (Package: com.roguedraft.game) and LottoCol: Loterías de Colombia. By downloading, accessing, or playing our games, you consent to the information collection and handling practices described herein.

2. Information We Collect and Process

We believe in data minimization. Depending on how you interact with our games, we may collect the following categories of information:

A. Automatically Collected Device & Technical Data

  • Device Identifiers: Mobile advertising identifiers (Google Advertising ID - GAID, IDFA), device model, operating system version, screen resolution, and system language.
  • Performance & Diagnostics: Anonymous crash logs, frame rate statistics, and session duration to ensure smooth 60 FPS gameplay and stability.
  • Network & Location: IP address (used strictly to derive coarse country/region for localization and regional ad compliance; precise GPS is never requested).

B. Gameplay & Progression Data

  • Leaderboard Records: High scores, player chosen nicknames, and daily run participation stored securely in Google Cloud Firestore.
  • Local Game State: Player levels, unlocked cosmetics, and sound preferences stored locally on your device via PlayerPrefs with cryptographic hash validation.

C. In-App Purchase Records

  • Purchases (such as Patronage Tiers and Ad-Free upgrades) are processed directly by Google Play In-App Billing. MazoteSoft never receives or stores your credit card number, bank details, or billing address. We only verify the digital transaction receipt to grant your permanent entitlements.

4. European Privacy Rights (GDPR / UK Data Protection)

If you are located in the European Economic Area (EEA) or the United Kingdom, you are entitled to specific rights under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR):

  • Consent Management (UMP): In accordance with Google's EU User Consent Policy, our games implement the certified Google User Messaging Platform (UMP). When launching the app, EEA/UK users are presented with a clear consent form allowing them to consent to, reject, or customize personalized advertising.
  • Revocation of Consent: You may modify your consent choices at any time through the in-game Settings panel or by resetting your device's Advertising ID.
  • Your Rights: You have the right to request access to, rectification of, or deletion of your personal data, as well as the right to object to processing.

5. California Consumer Privacy Rights (CCPA / CPRA)

For California residents, the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA) provides the right to know what personal information is collected, request its deletion, and opt out of the "sale" or "sharing" of personal data:

  • We Do Not Sell Personal Information: MazoteSoft does not sell personal data for monetary compensation.
  • Opt-Out of Interest-Based Ads: You can opt out of cross-context behavioral advertising directly on your Android device via Settings > Google > Ads > Delete advertising ID or Opt out of Ads Personalization.

6. Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

Our games (including Rogue Draft) are designed for general audiences and are not directed to children under 13 years of age (or under 16 in the European Union). We do not knowingly collect personal identifiable information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us immediately and we will take prompt steps to remove such data.

7. Data Security and Retention

We implement technical and organizational security measures to protect your data, including TLS/HTTPS encryption for all cloud network transmissions and client-side cryptographic hashing for game state integrity. We retain anonymous leaderboard scores only for as long as necessary to maintain active competition ladders.

8. Updates to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our games, technology, or legal requirements. Any modifications will be posted directly on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date.

Operamos bajo el principio de minimización de datos. Según tu interacción con nuestras aplicaciones, podemos procesar:

A. Datos Técnicos y del Dispositivo Recopilados Automáticamente

  • Identificadores de Dispositivo: Identificador de publicidad móvil (Google Advertising ID - GAID, IDFA), modelo del dispositivo, versión del sistema operativo, resolución de pantalla e idioma configurado.

B. Datos de Progresión y Juego

  • Récords de Clasificación: Puntuaciones máximas, apodo elegido por el jugador y participación en el Desafío Diario, sincronizados de forma anónima en Google Cloud Firestore.

C. Registro de Compras en la Aplicación

  • Las transacciones de tiers de mecenazgo y eliminación de anuncios se procesan de forma segura y directa por Google Play In-App Billing. MazoteSoft nunca tiene acceso ni almacena números de tarjeta de crédito ni cuentas bancarias. Únicamente validamos el recibo digital para activar tus ventajas permanentes.
